## Deliberate assumptions

- Senior software engineer is the primary umbrella; payments and platforms lead the story. Mobile and ERP are supporting evidence, not six competing headline job titles.
- The existing target is a user Pages site, `donnc.github.io`; no custom domain has been assumed.
- Projects with substantial recent work qualify even if first created before the three-year cutoff. The eight cases have recent activity; dates are not claims of employment tenure.
- Public source links are optional and are secondary to internal case studies. Professional work can be showcased as an engineering narrative without source disclosure.
- Product maturity is unspecified when not confirmed. No user counts, performance percentages, certifications, banking licenses or deployment assertions are invented.
- WCE and ERP dates track recent activity in their grouped project families, not launch dates. Keep them aligned with substantive work during updates.

## Critical assessment

The site makes domain breadth and personal ownership much clearer. However, case studies currently describe capabilities more strongly than measured outcomes. Senior/lead credibility would improve substantially with one approved artifact and one verifiable outcome for each of mPOS/mTMS, workflows and AFC Mobile. The active PMP supports delivery discipline; it does not itself establish engineering seniority.

KinetiFrame sits later in the work collection so its WIP status enriches the story without competing with the banking and payment evidence. The home shows three cases to avoid overwhelming a recruiter. The remaining five are one click away.

All diagrams are labelled system sketches and are conceptual, not product screenshots or exact internal topology. No CV download is shown because a current CV was not supplied. No LinkedIn URL is guessed because the existing profile and scaffold used different addresses.
