## Bring a story to life, without redrawing every move
**Kinetiframe is my flagship creative product:** a mobile-first 2D animation studio for Android. It brings frame-by-frame drawing and rigged character animation into the same creative workflow.

The idea came from a personal frustration. I wanted to tell animated stories, but drawing the same character again just to lift a hand or change an expression made every small action expensive. Actor Studio grew from that problem: create a character, give it movement, and reuse that work in your next scene.

Draw when you want something new. Pose when the character already exists. Build the scene around the story you want to tell.

## Actor Studio: characters you can perform with
Actor Studio gives a character independently authored front, profile, three-quarter and back views. Each view has its own skeleton, artwork and part stacking, so a side-facing walk uses artwork designed for that perspective.

Creators pose characters through joint controls, shape movement over time and save performances for reuse. Human, quadruped and custom structures support different characters and articulated props. Motion includes pose timing and movement through the scene, with view-specific presets that can be auditioned and adjusted.

Performances reference a specific actor revision. Improving a character therefore does not silently change the artwork behind an earlier saved performance.

## Drawing and reusable assets belong together
Frame-by-frame layers carry original drawings and expression changes. Actors carry repeatable poses and movement. Mouth, eye, hand and prop packs provide artwork a creator can place and reuse rather than draw from scratch every time.

The goal is creative flexibility: a presenter gesturing at a desk, a side-facing character running, or a scene mixing drawings, moving actors and a background. Reuse saves effort without requiring every scene to follow the same template.

## Scene composition and camera
The studio brings animation together with backgrounds, audio and embedded video. Camera controls support framing, movement and actor-follow, while reusable camera presets help carry a shot's intent across projects.

The creative workflow connects character preparation, performance, scene composition and the timeline. Editable project and pack formats preserve the material behind the result, so it can be revisited and reused.

## The engineering behind the experience
I work across product design, native Android interaction, drawing and deformation maths, motion evaluation, persistence and media handling. Editor previews, thumbnails and project export share actor-rendering logic to keep the interpretation of a character consistent.

Disk-backed frames, bounded caches and streamed audio processing address mobile resource constraints. The architecture supports the creative workflow, rather than making the creator think about its implementation.

## A product I own and intend to grow
Kinetiframe is a product I am building a business around. I own its creative direction, user experience and engineering, with a commercial goal centred on useful animation tools and the time reusable artwork and performances save creators.

That direction shapes my decisions: make local creation useful, make reusable work valuable, and give people a clear reason to return to the studio.
